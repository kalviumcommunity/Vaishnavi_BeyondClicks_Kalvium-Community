# Narrative Feedback and Edits

## Reviewer

Peer / Friend / Family Member

## Questions Asked

1. What is the main finding in this analysis?
2. What should the company do about it?
3. Was anything confusing?

## Feedback

The reviewer understood that customers who wait longer for support are more likely to churn. They also understood that the recommended solution is to improve support response time.

The reviewer suggested keeping the explanation focused on business impact instead of technical terminology.

## Changes Made

Based on the feedback:

- Simplified technical language.
- Highlighted the 3% versus 12% churn comparison.
- Made the recommendations more specific.
- Added owners and timelines.
- Explained why faster support matters to customers.
- Kept the narrative structure as Context → Data → Finding → Why → Action.
