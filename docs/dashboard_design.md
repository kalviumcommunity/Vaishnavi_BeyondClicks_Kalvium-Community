# BeyondClicks Dashboard Design Documentation

## Project

BeyondClicks - Marketing Campaign Activation Analytics Dashboard

The dashboard is designed using a four-level information hierarchy:
Status → Trends → Segments → Detail.

---

## Information Hierarchy Applied

### Level 1 - Status

The top section contains five KPI cards.

#### 1. Total Revenue
Shows the total revenue generated by the selected campaigns.

Business question:

> How much revenue are our campaigns generating?

#### 2. Activated Users
Shows the number of users who became activated users.

Business question:

> Are our campaigns generating meaningful user activation?

#### 3. Average CTR
Shows the average Click-Through Rate.

Business question:

> How effectively are campaigns generating clicks from impressions?

#### 4. Activation Rate
Shows the percentage of users who become activated.

Business question:

> How effectively are campaigns converting users into activated users?

#### 5. Average ROI
Shows the average return on investment.

Business question:

> Are campaign investments producing sufficient returns?

Each KPI displays a current value and period-over-period percentage change.

---

## Level 2 - Trends

Three time-series charts are displayed below the KPI cards.

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

Shows how campaign revenue changes over time.

Business question:

> Is revenue increasing or decreasing over time?

### 2. Monthly CTR Trend

Shows changes in click-through rate over time.

Business question:

> Is campaign engagement improving?

### 3. Monthly Activation Rate Trend

Shows how activation performance changes over time.

Business question:

> Are campaigns becoming more effective at activating users?

---

## Level 3 - Segment Analysis

The dashboard contains two comparison charts.

### Revenue by Customer Segment

Shows total revenue generated by each customer segment.

Business question:

> Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?

### Average ROI by Campaign Type

Shows the average ROI for each campaign type.

Business question:

> Which campaign types provide better returns?

Bar charts are used because they make comparisons between categories easy.

---

## Level 4 - Detailed Campaign Explorer

The detailed section provides progressive disclosure.

Users can filter the data using:

- Customer Segment
- Campaign Type
- Platform
- Date Range

After applying filters, the dashboard displays the matching campaign records.

Users can also download the filtered data as a CSV file.

This allows users to move from high-level business metrics into detailed investigation only when required.

---

## Design Principles Applied

### 1. Progressive Disclosure

Important summary information is visible immediately.

Detailed campaign records are available below the summary and can be narrowed using filters.

### 2. Spatial Organisation

The most important KPIs are placed at the top of the dashboard.

The user can scan from:

Status → Trends → Segments → Details.

### 3. Consistent Visual Language

The dashboard uses consistent chart formatting and KPI indicators.

Positive and negative changes are communicated consistently through Streamlit metric indicators.

### 4. Context Over Numbers

KPI values are accompanied by period-over-period changes.

Trend charts provide historical context for the metrics.

---

## Target Audience

### Marketing VP

Uses the KPI summary and trends to understand overall campaign performance.

### Sales Director

Uses segment and campaign-type analysis to identify strong-performing areas.

### CEO

Can quickly scan the KPI cards to determine whether the business is on track.

### Analysts

Can use filters, detailed records and CSV export for deeper investigation.

---

## Data Source

The dashboard uses:

`data/processed/feature_engineered_campaign_data.csv`

The dataset contains 55,555 campaign records and 23 columns.

Feature-engineered metrics used by the dashboard include:

- CTR
- Signup Rate
- Activation Rate
- Click-to-Activation Rate
- Revenue per Activated User
- Cost per Activated User
- Cost per Signup

---

## Dashboard Flow

The dashboard follows this information hierarchy:

1. **Status** - Are we on track?
2. **Trends** - Is performance getting better or worse?
3. **Segments** - Which areas need attention?
4. **Detail** - Why is something happening?

This structure prevents users from being overwhelmed by raw campaign data while still providing detailed analysis when required.
